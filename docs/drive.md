# Google Drive scoping (Phase 2)

- **Two modes** (`ATLAS_DRIVE_AUTH`): `oauth` (default) acts through users' own Google logins; `service_account` uses the Firebase service account — only viable when the parent is a **Google Workspace Shared Drive** (files are owned by the drive) with the SA added as *Content manager*, because Google gives service accounts zero My Drive storage, so Atlas acts through **users' own Google OAuth** (the Gmail connection now also requests the `drive` scope). `ATLAS_DRIVE_PARENT_FOLDER_ID` is your "Open Cowork" folder; `ATLAS_DRIVE_OWNER_UID` is your Atlas uid, and `ATLAS_DRIVE_OWNER_EMAIL` names which of your connected Google accounts owns that folder.
- **On signup** (`user.provision` → `drive.provision`): *your* token creates `<parent>/<slug>/` and `<parent>/<slug>/inbox/` (you stay owner) and shares `<slug>/` with the user's Google identities (writer). IDs are stored on `users/{uid}.provisioning`. If you haven't granted Drive yet, Linux provisioning still succeeds and the Drive step is retried from Settings or `/admin`.
- **Scope enforcement:** sync runs with the **user's own token** (their quota, their files), falling back to yours; the brain only ever addresses a user's `driveFolderId` subtree. Drive permissions themselves keep users out of each other's folders.
- **Pull** every 15 min (`n8n/06-drive.json`): new/changed files (by md5) under `<slug>/<sub>/` → `~/projects/<sub>/`, `<slug>/inbox/` and loose files → `~/projects/inbox/`. A Drive subfolder becomes an Atlas project automatically. Google Docs/Sheets/Slides export as `.md`/`.csv`/`.pdf`. Files are written **as the user** via `atlas-run`, so ownership is theirs.
- **Push** hourly: files created or changed on the Pi in `projects/*` (≤ 25 MB, not dotfiles) upload to the matching Drive folder; unchanged files are skipped by md5. The map lives in `users/{uid}/driveFiles/{fileId}`.
- **Moving** a file between projects (Phase 3) updates both sides using this map.
